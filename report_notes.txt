RESULTS - Differential expression

After filtering, 27357 probes were tested. No probe remained significant after
Benjamini-Hochberg correction for multiple testing (smallest adjusted p-value = 0.22).
Because the standard threshold (adjusted p < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 0.5) yielded fewer
than 20 probes, an exploratory threshold of nominal p < 0.01 and |log2FC| > 0.5 was
applied, which identified 241 probes. Of these, 174 had higher expression in DHF and
67 had lower expression. The probes with the smallest p-values corresponded to EHD3,
PAK6, CCL25, MOXD1 and SEC24D (Table 1, Figure 2). These results are exploratory
and should be interpreted with caution.

Figure 2 caption: Volcano plot of differential expression between DHF (n = 10) and
DF (n = 18) acute-phase samples. Each point is a probe. The x-axis shows the log2
fold change (DHF minus DF) and the y-axis shows -log10 of the nominal p-value. Red
points passed the exploratory threshold (nominal p < 0.01, |log2FC| > 0.5).

DISCUSSION NOTE
With 28 patients and thousands of tests, the study had low statistical power. The
number of probes with p < 0.01 (241) is close to the number expected by chance
(about 1% of the probes tested), so the individual genes reported here should be
regarded as hypotheses, not validated markers.

RESULTS - Classifier

A LASSO logistic regression classifier was evaluated with 20 repeats of stratified
5-fold cross-validation on the 28 acute samples (18 DF, 10 DHF), using the 5,000
most variable probes. Feature selection took place inside each training fold. The
classifier did not discriminate DHF from DF: the mean area under the ROC curve
(AUC) was 0.52 (95% interval across repeats 0.39-0.68), which is indistinguishable
from chance (AUC = 0.5) (Figure 3). When the model was fitted on all samples with
the conservative regularisation setting (lambda.1se), LASSO retained no probes,
so no minimal gene panel could be defined.

Figure 3 caption: ROC curve for the LASSO classifier of DHF versus DF from repeat 1
of the cross-validation. The diagonal line represents random guessing (AUC = 0.5).

DISCUSSION NOTES
- Hypothesis not supported: AUC above 0.80 with fewer than 10 genes was not reached.
- Consistent with the differential expression: no probe passed multiple-testing
  correction (smallest adjusted p = 0.22).
- Possible reasons: small sample (10 DHF, 18 DF), whole blood mixes many cell types,
  sampling days 2-9 after onset (the immune response changes quickly), a single
  hospital and season, and DF and DHF may differ in the clinical course more than
  in gene expression at presentation.
- Strength: nested cross-validation with feature selection inside the folds avoids
  over-optimistic results.
- Implication: with this dataset, a gene panel cannot yet support early detection
  of severe dengue; larger multi-site cohorts and samples taken before the
  critical phase are needed.

RESULTS - Pathway enrichment

The 241 probes with nominal p < 0.01 and |log2FC| > 0.5 corresponded to 185 unique
gene symbols. These were tested for Gene Ontology (biological process) enrichment
using clusterProfiler (Yu et al., 2012) against a background of 13,288 genes
represented on the array. Five terms were significantly enriched (Benjamini-Hochberg
adjusted p < 0.05), all related to protein handling in the endoplasmic reticulum
(ER): ER-associated degradation (ERAD) pathway (8 genes, adjusted p = 0.005),
protein exit from the ER (6 genes, adjusted p = 0.005), retrograde protein transport
from ER to cytosol (5 genes, adjusted p = 0.005), ER-to-cytosol transport (5 genes,
adjusted p = 0.005) and regulation of protein exit from the ER (4 genes, adjusted
p = 0.042) (Figure 4, Table 2). Because these terms share many of the same genes,
they represent one biological theme rather than five independent findings.

DISCUSSION NOTES
- Theme: the ER protein-quality-control system (ERAD, ER exit). Dengue virus makes
  its proteins and replicates on ER membranes, and ER stress responses are widely
  reported in dengue, so this theme is biologically plausible. Find one paper that
  supports this (search Google Scholar: "dengue virus endoplasmic reticulum ERAD")
  and cite it.
- Caution: the gene list comes from nominal p-values, and the number of probes
  (241) is about what chance would give. The enrichment correction accounts for
  testing many GO terms, but not for the noise in the gene list. So this is a
  hypothesis for future testing, not a confirmed mechanism.
- The enrichment shows that the weak signal is not random noise in biology terms,
  but it was not strong enough to give a working classifier (AUC 0.52).
