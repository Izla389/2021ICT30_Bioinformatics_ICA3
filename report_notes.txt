RESULTS - Differential expression

After filtering, 27357 probes were tested. No probe remained significant after
Benjamini-Hochberg correction for multiple testing (smallest adjusted p-value = 0.22).
Because the standard threshold (adjusted p < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 0.5) yielded fewer
than 20 probes, an exploratory threshold of nominal p < 0.01 and |log2FC| > 0.5 was
applied, which identified 241 probes. Of these, 174 had higher expression in DHF and
67 had lower expression. The probes with the smallest p-values corresponded to EHD3,
PAK6, CCL25, MOXD1 and SEC24D (Table 1, Figure 2). These results are exploratory
and should be interpreted with caution.

Figure 2 caption: Volcano plot of differential expression between DHF (n = 10) and
DF (n = 18) acute-phase samples. Each point is a probe. The x-axis shows the log2
fold change (DHF minus DF) and the y-axis shows -log10 of the nominal p-value. Red
points passed the exploratory threshold (nominal p < 0.01, |log2FC| > 0.5).

DISCUSSION NOTE
With 28 patients and thousands of tests, the study had low statistical power. The
number of probes with p < 0.01 (241) is close to the number expected by chance
(about 1% of the probes tested), so the individual genes reported here should be
regarded as hypotheses, not validated markers.
